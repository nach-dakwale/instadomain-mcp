How it works

From "I need a domain" to "it's live" in one thread.

InstaDomain gives your coding assistant the full domain workflow, so the boring registrar steps disappear into the background while you keep building.

1

Connect the MCP server once

Point Claude, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible client at InstaDomain. That unlocks domain search, suggestions, checkout, and post-purchase status in the same interface you already use.

2

Tell your AI what you need

Ask for ideas, have it compare options, or name a specific domain. Your assistant handles the tedious part: availability checks, pricing, and choosing the next move without bouncing you into registrar UX.

3

Your domain is live with DNS ready

Stripe checkout completes the order, OpenSRS registers the domain, and Cloudflare creates the zone automatically with a scoped API token your agent can use right away.

What your AI can do

Give your assistant real domain-buying leverage.

These are not passive API endpoints sitting in docs. They are capabilities your assistant can actively use while helping you ship.

check_domainAnswer "can we get this?" instantly

Your AI can check a single domain on demand and show exact pricing before you commit.

check_domains_bulkCompare a whole short list in one pass

Have it screen up to 50 options at once instead of checking names manually one by one.

suggest_domainsBrainstorm names that are actually available

Give a keyword and your AI can generate ideas, check them, and surface the viable ones instead of hallucinating dead ends.

buy_domainTurn a decision into a purchase

Once you pick a name, your assistant can kick off Stripe checkout and finish the registration flow without sending you into registrar upsells.

get_domain_statusConfirm registration and DNS are ready

Your AI can verify the domain was registered, report DNS status, and hand back the details you need after the order clears.

After purchaseWHOIS privacy and Cloudflare setup included

Checkout flows into OpenSRS registration, then a Cloudflare zone is created automatically with a scoped API token for ongoing DNS management.

What happens after checkout

Stripe, OpenSRS, and Cloudflare are stitched into one flow.

When your AI starts a purchase, checkout runs through Stripe. After payment, InstaDomain registers the domain through OpenSRS, creates a Cloudflare DNS zone automatically, and returns a scoped API token for managing that zone. WHOIS privacy is included where the registry supports it.
